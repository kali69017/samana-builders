FAQs about Buildertrend’s sales management features

a

What sales management tools are included with my Buildertrend subscription?

Buildertrend includes a full set of sales tools – CRM Hub, Proposals with digital signatures and Email Marketing – along with project management, financial and communication tools, all included in your subscription from day one. Everything works together in one platform, so your sales process connects seamlessly to the build without add-ons or upgrades.

a

Can Buildertrend replace my existing CRM or sales tools?

Yes. If you’re looking for a system purpose-built for contractors managing multiple projects and complex budgets, Buildertrend’s CRM Hub, Proposals and Email Marketing work together the way they should – connected to your entire business, not isolated from it. You’ll actually see how sales connects to profitability.

a

Can I reuse proposals, estimates and emails to save time?

Yes. Buildertrend allows you to create reusable proposal, estimate and email marketing templates, so you can respond faster, stay consistent and reduce manual work. This helps ensure more accurate pricing and a more professional experience at every stage of the sales process.

a

How does Buildertrend support my sales process from lead to contract?

From first contact to signed contract, everything lives in one system. Your CRM captures every lead and conversation. When you send a proposal, it’s tied to that lead and tracks whether it’s been opened, read or signed. Your team doesn’t re-enter data. They execute. Your financials are accurate from day one because the estimate and the project are the same thing. This is how serious builders scale without adding overhead.

a

How quickly can I start managing leads and sending proposals in Buildertrend?

You can start capturing leads and sending professional proposals quickly with guided setup and onboarding. Buildertrend’s tools are designed to be easy to adopt, helping your team build a consistent sales process without slowing down day-to-day operations.

a

What happens to our data when we transition from another system?

We handle the migration. Your historical data gets brought in safely. Your team gets trained on the new workflow. You’re not starting from scratch. You’re building on what you’ve already captured.

a

Does Buildertrend integrate with other sales software?

We integrate with HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive. If you’re already invested in one of those, we can sync your data. If you’re already using one of those tools, your data syncs automatically between your CRM and Buildertrend. Your leads, contacts and deal information stay current across both systems in real time.
