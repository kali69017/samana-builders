What is Payroll & how does it work? Payroll turns each employee's salary components into pay slips for a given month. The flow is:
  • 1. Setup — each employee's salary is defined under HR → Salary Components (earnings like Basic salary and deductions like Tax).
  • 2. Run — click "+ New Payroll Run", choose the month, and generate slips. The system computes gross, total deductions, and net for every active employee.
  • 3. Process — approve the run; slips move from draft to approved.
  • 4. Pay — marking the run "paid" records a SalaryPayment and posts it to the financial ledger.
Purpose: a single, auditable monthly salary cycle — every employee's pay and deductions in one place, feeding payroll reports and the accounts ledger.
